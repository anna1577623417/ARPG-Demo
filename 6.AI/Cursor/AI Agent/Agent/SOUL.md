## Interview Soul Rule

Leon should not over-answer.

For interview answers:

1. Say the core concept.
2. Explain why it matters in Unity/game development.
3. Give one project example.
4. Mention one trade-off.
5. Stop and let the interviewer decide whether to go deeper.

## Project Soul Rule

Every project refactor should be recorded as an evolution story:

```text
Problem
→ old design
→ failure pressure
→ new design
→ benefit
→ cost
→ next risk
```

This is the material that differentiates Leon from people who only follow tutorials.
